A

Crownfall

Regal and cinematic. The logo is the hero.

Palette

  • Void#07030f
  • Royal#2a0f5c
  • Crown gold#d9b863
  • Candle#f4e3ad

one color per crown spike, in the game's own order

Type

Marcellus, carved capitals

Hanken Grotesk for reading text.

Signature 3D moment

Your crown-W cast in gold, turning in a dark hall. On scroll it breaks into its five spikes. Each takes one of the five colors and leads a chapter of the page.

Scroll story

  1. A dark hall. The gold mark turns slowly under five beams.
  2. The mark splits into five spikes and the camera follows them down.
  3. Who we are, and the two nights.
  4. Members as gold-framed portraits along the hall.
  5. Events: the weekly raffle and the end-of-quarter giveaway.
  6. The spikes rejoin into the crown above the Discord invite.

Cost and risk

Build: medium. The logo is already traced to a vector, which is the hard part of making it 3D.

Risk: it is about the emblem more than the people, and dark fantasy openers are common.

Phones: a still gold mark with a light sweep.

B

Pod Night

Warm and social. The table is the hero.

Palette

  • Espresso#17100c
  • Felt#3d1d7a
  • Lamp#ffb84d
  • Card stock#f3e7d3
  • Friday#39d0c0

Type

Bricolage Grotesque

Caveat for life totals and scribbles

Hanken Grotesk for reading text.

Signature 3D moment

A four-seat Commander table built from simple shapes: felt, playmats, card stacks, a d20. The camera circles it as you scroll. Three seats are taken. The fourth is left open for the visitor.

Scroll story

  1. 6 PM. A lamp comes on over an empty table with the mark stitched into the felt.
  2. The camera drops to the first seat: who we are.
  3. Second seat: the two nights. The light turns amber for Thursday at Iruda Cafe and teal for Friday at Silver King.
  4. Third seat: members are dealt onto the table as cards you can flip.
  5. Center of the table: raffle tickets and the giveaway deck box.
  6. The fourth seat is empty. It is yours, and it holds the Discord invite.

Cost and risk

Build: highest. A full scene with lighting and a camera path, though every object is a simple shape with no modeling software needed.

Risk: needs the most tuning to hold 60fps.

Phones: a layered illustration of the table with parallax.

C

Top Deck

Bright and collectible. The card is the interface.

Palette

  • Sleeve white#f1edf9
  • Ink#1b1033
  • Husky purple#4b2e83
  • Foil gold#c9a23f
  • Holo foilgradient

Type

Anton, loud and condensed

JetBrains Mono for collector numbers, 001/100

Hanken Grotesk for reading text.

Signature 3D moment

A 100-card deck in the middle of the screen. Scrolling shuffles it and draws cards off the top. Each card flips to fill the screen and becomes a section.

Scroll story

  1. A sealed deck with the mark on its back. Scroll cuts and shuffles it.
  2. The top card flips: who we are.
  3. Next draw: the two nights, as two cards side by side.
  4. Members spread out like a binder page of foil cards that tilt under the pointer.
  5. Events go on the stack: the weekly raffle, then the giveaway as the rare.
  6. Card 100 of 100 is blank with your name on it, next to the Discord invite.

Cost and risk

Build: lowest. Much of it works with CSS 3D, so it also runs well on phones.

Risk: the least three.js spectacle of the three, unless the shuffle is built in WebGL.

Phones: nearly the full experience, with tilt driven by touch.

The same in all three

  • The purple and gold of your logo anchor the palette.
  • Member cards with foil and pointer tilt, as shown in C. They are the best home for the member list in any direction.
  • Five accent colors in the game's order: white, blue, black, red, green. Original colors and shapes only, no official symbols or card art.
  • Club, member and event content read from plain data files that officers can edit.
  • A calm version for visitors who turn off motion, and the fan content disclaimer in the footer.
Recommendation

I would build B, Pod Night

It is the only direction about the club itself: people around a table, at two real places, at 6 PM. The open fourth seat turns the whole page into an invitation, which is the job of the site. It is also the biggest build, and the assignment rewards aiming high.

I would bring two things across from the others: the foil member cards from C, and the gold crown from A as the object the lamp lights at the center of the table.

Reply with A, B or C, or name the parts you want mixed.
